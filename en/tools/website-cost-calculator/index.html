How to read the estimate

The calculation starts from a base per project type and applies four factors: pages beyond the ones already included, integrations with external systems, design drawn from scratch, and the additional items you tick. The result is an interval, not a number: the distance between the minimum and the maximum is the space in which the scope can reasonably change without changing the nature of the work.

Treat it as reliable within 30%. If reality sits below the minimum, you are usually asking for something more standardised than what you selected. If it sits above the maximum, there is almost always one integration nobody has documented yet: the line item that moves more figures across quotes than any other.

The figures are Italian market ranges, VAT excluded. This is not a TiTech price list and contains none of our prices: the full range table, line by line, is in the article on website costs in Italy, which is also where you find what each band usually excludes.

What the calculator does not know. It cannot see the quality of your content, the tidiness of your product database, the decisions you have not made yet. Those are exactly the three things that, in discovery, move the number, usually more than everything you can drag here.

Questions about the estimate

Is the estimate a quote?

No, and it does not want to become one. It is an order of magnitude built on market ranges: it tells you whether a project sits in a certain class of spend, so you do not discover on a call that the distance between you and the supplier is a factor of ten. The quote comes later: separate line items, what is included, what is excluded, deadlines.

Is what I enter saved or sent anywhere?

Neither. The calculation happens in the browser with a few lines of JavaScript, there is no field to fill in and nothing is shipped to a server. You can get your range and leave without a trace.

Why such a wide band?

Because the range is the honest datum. A business site with finished texts and a business site that has to integrate with a 2011 management system share a name and very few working hours. A single number at this stage would be false with reassuring precision.

Does it work for e-commerce too?

Yes, with one warning: in the calculation "pages" become product listings, and twenty listings populate a catalogue while two hundred are a data project. The critical line of an online shop is not the number of rows in the database, it is how well that database is populated.

And maintenance?

When you tick it, it appears as a separate yearly cost, because that is exactly how it belongs in a budget: updating, backing up and keeping certificates valid is work that continues after launch. It can also stay in-house, provided somebody genuinely does it.
