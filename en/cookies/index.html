Cookie policy

This site uses no profiling cookies and sets no cookies of its own. That is why there is no banner: asking for consent that serves no purpose is an annoyance, not a protection.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a site stores in the browser while you navigate. They exist to remember information between one page and the next, or one visit and the next: a language preference, a cart, a logged-in session.

There are technologies with similar effects (local storage inside the browser, tracking pixels), and the rules are the same: when they are not strictly necessary to provide the service the user asked for, they require consent.

None of it is used for advertising. Active

How the banner works

Consent is required for tools that are not strictly necessary to provide the service you asked for. On this site there is one: Google Analytics. Until you press Accept, no request goes to Google and no statistics cookie is written.

Your choice stays in your browser. If you refuse, the site behaves exactly the same: we only measure the page views of people who accepted, which means the numbers we read are part of the total, not the total. To change your mind, clear the site data in your browser and the banner appears again.

We did not add Analytics out of habit. It serves one purpose: working out which pages bring business and which do not, so we can remove the second kind.

How to manage cookies in your browser

Even though this site sets no cookie, you can control those of the other sites you visit from your browser settings. In the privacy and security sections of Chrome, Safari, Firefox and Edge you find the options to block third-party cookies, delete them when you close the browser or erase data already stored.

Blocking third-party cookies does not affect the use of this site. Blocking external fonts may instead change the look of the typefaces slightly: the text stays perfectly readable, and a system font is used.

Processing of personal data

The purposes, legal bases, retention periods and the rights you can exercise are described in the privacy policy. For any question write to info@titechdigital.it.
