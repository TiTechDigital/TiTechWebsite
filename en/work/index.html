Platform · Public healthcare2022

Digital healthcare initiative designed to modernize patient queue management in public hospitals. What a case study contains

A useful case is not a gallery of screenshots. It has to let a reader recognise their own situation and judge whether the result is repeatable.

The context

Who the client is, what they sell, how the work ran before. The information that makes the problem understandable to someone outside the sector.

The constraint

The fact that determined every other choice: a management system nobody wanted to touch, a catalogue of thousands of items, a team with no technical skills.

The decision

What we built and, above all, what we dropped. The alternatives we considered explain more than the final screen does.

The result

A measurable number: enquiries a month, time saved, orders, load time. If the number does not exist yet, we say so instead of inventing one.

Your project could be the next case

If you have a problem that resembles one of these, write to us. If it looks nothing like them, that is more interesting still: it usually means the conversation is worth having.
