Five ways to solve a digital problem

Not every business problem is solved with a new website. Sometimes you need an online store, sometimes a piece of software that removes one manual step. Here are the five areas we work in, with what each one really involves.

Describe what you need How we work

The areas of work

Each page describes what the work includes, how it runs and what we can stand behind. If your case sits halfway between two areas that isn't a problem: it happens often.

01

Website design

The company website that explains what you do, reassures the people assessing you and brings in quote requests. Fast, accessible, manageable without coming back to us for every sentence.

02

E-commerce

Selling online when the catalogue, the payments and the logistics make the channel sustainable. Catalogue, checkout, shipping, integration with the management system.

03

Custom software

Internal and external web applications: orders, bookings, client portals, dashboards. When standard software costs more in workarounds than in licences.

04

Automation and AI

Repetitive steps moved off the spreadsheet and into an automated flow: quotes, document classification, first-level replies, synchronisation between systems.

05

SEO

The technical and content work that makes the site findable on Google: structure, speed, structured data, pages that answer real questions.

Where you start

In most cases the starting point is not the technology but a question: what has to change inside the company once this work is finished?

If the answer is "more serious quote requests", the work is about the website and the content. If it is "selling outside the shop as well", it is about online commerce and logistics. If it is "stop re-typing the same data by hand", the work is a piece of software or an automation.

Settling that before writing any code avoids the most expensive case of all: a project that succeeds technically and changes no number in the business.

  • First the analysis and the definition of scope, then a written proposal.
  • No project starts without agreed objectives and criteria for success.
  • If the right solution is smaller than the one you asked for, we say so.
