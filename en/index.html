From problem to product

A transparent process keeps goals, technical decisions and results aligned. Every phase has a clear purpose and prepares the next one.

  1. Analysis

    We define goals, users, constraints and existing systems before choosing the technical direction.

  2. Design

    We shape the experience and architecture, sharing priorities and decisions with the client.

  3. Development

    We build the solution, test its functionality and prepare a reliable release.

  4. Evolution

    We observe how it is used, gather new needs and plan the next improvements.

Common questions

What kind of solutions do you build?

Websites, e-commerce, web applications, custom software, UI/UX design, technical SEO, AI and automation, ongoing support.

Which businesses do you work with?

We work with SMEs, established companies, startups and organizations that want to modernize their processes and digital tools. We operate from Italy with an international outlook and a particular focus on the Albanian market.

How does a collaboration start?

We start by discussing goals, the people involved and technical constraints. From there we define a direction and the project steps.

Can you integrate with the systems we already use?

Yes. We analyze the existing infrastructure and tools to understand how to integrate them and where it makes sense to intervene.

How do you define timelines and costs?

Once goals and scope are clear, we share a proposal with activities, phases and investment before we start.

What happens after launch?

Launch is not the end of the project. We provide maintenance, technical support and continuous optimization to support the product over time.
