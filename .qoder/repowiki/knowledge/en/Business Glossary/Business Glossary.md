### I nostri lavori
- Definition：The portfolio section of the TiTech Digital site, positioned between the studio block and the FAQ. It displays six demo projects in a dark editorial two-column grid, each card showing an image, year badge, numbered line, name, description and tags. The section is wired into the top nav, mobile menu, in-page index and footer Explore column.
- Aliases：lavori、portfolio section

### Progetti dimostrativi
- Definition：Placeholder/demo projects (Aurora Ceramiche, Ferro e Forma, LogiTrack, Verde Casa, Officina Nova, Casa Marani) that populate the `#lavori` list. They carry a visible note (`lavori__note`) marking them as samples and must be replaced with real client work before publication, along with updating the selected-project counter.
- Aliases：casi fittizi、demo projects

### TiTech Digital
- Definition：The company this site represents — a technology partner for businesses based in Italy with an international horizon and particular attention to the Albanian market. The site documents its positioning across web/e-commerce, digital design, software/automation, SEO/growth and ongoing support.
