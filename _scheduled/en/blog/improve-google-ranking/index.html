How to improve a website's ranking on Google

Improving ranking is not a formula: it is work on four areas, done in a sensible order. Here is what they mean, what to do first and which shortcuts to avoid.

Talk about your project SEO consulting

Enea MujaCEO, TiTech Digital · 8 min readUpdated 7 December 2026

In brief

  • Four areas. Technical, content, experience, authority.
  • Technical base first. If Google cannot read the site well, the rest is pointless.
  • One page for each question. Pages made for what people search.
  • No shortcuts. Bought links and mass-produced text do harm.
  1. 01The four areas
  2. 02What to do first
  3. 03What not to do
  4. 04Measure and wait

The four areas

To look after in this order.

  • 1. Technical SEOThe site can be read and indexed: sitemap, clean addresses, no page blocked by mistake.
  • 2. Content and intentEach page answers a precise question, with titles and text made for that search.
  • 3. ExperienceSpeed and phone use: see slow website.
  • 4. AuthorityLinks from other serious sites and real reviews: it grows over time.

What to do first

A working order.

  • Check indexing in Google Search Console.
  • Pick one keyword per page and revise title, description and text.
  • Fix speed and phone use.
  • Link pages together with sensible internal links.
  • Look after the Google profile if you work in an area: local SEO.

What not to do

Three shortcuts that cost dearly.

  • Buying links: Google recognises them and can penalise the site.
  • Mass-produced pages with the same text and a different city name.
  • Stuffing text with keywords.

Measure and wait

Results are not immediate.

Look in Search Console at which searches you appear for, how often and in what position, and adjust. Nobody can guarantee a position on Google: you build the right foundations and measure the result over time. For how we work see SEO consulting.

Frequently asked questions

How do I improve my ranking on Google?

By looking after the technical base, pages made for what people search, speed and, over time, real links and reviews.

How long does it take to climb?

It depends on competition and starting point: from a few weeks to months. There is no guarantee.

Do I need backlinks?

They help in competitive searches, but they must be real and earned, not bought.

Can I do it myself?

The basics yes. For the technical side and competitive searches an expert helps.
