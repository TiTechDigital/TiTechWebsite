How much does website maintenance cost?

A website costs money after going live too: hosting, updates, backups and support. The fee varies a lot between suppliers, and comparing just the total misleads. Here is what matters and what the indicative values are.

In brief

  • Indicative value for a business site. Generally between 900 and 3,000 euros a year, all items included.
  • An e-commerce costs more. It can exceed 4,000 euros a year.
  • What is included matters. Activities, not just hours.
  • Ask before launch. The fee is agreed together with the project.

What the fee covers

Six items.

  • Hosting and domainWhere the site lives and its address.
  • UpdatesOf the system, components and libraries.
  • BackupsRegular and with a restore test.
  • Security and monitoringChecks and alerts if something is wrong.
  • Small changesText, hours, images.
  • SupportReply times when you need help.

Indicative values

From our estimates, not a price list.

Across the most common recurring items (hosting, domain, updates, backups, monitoring and support) a well-run business site generally costs between 900 and 3,000 euros a year; an e-commerce can exceed 4,000 euros. These are references, not offers: the value for your site depends on the site. The broader picture is in how much does a website cost in 2026.

What makes the fee change

Four factors.

  • Complexity of the site: components, integrations, custom features.
  • Traffic and criticality: an online store cannot stay down.
  • Reply times required.
  • How much you do yourself: if you can edit content, the fee drops.

How to compare two offers

Activities, not hours.

A contract that says "2 hours a month" says nothing about what happens when 2 hours are not enough. Ask for a list of activities, the reply times, what is excluded and whether the fee includes hosting and domain. What good maintenance includes: website maintenance. Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to maintain a website each year?

For a well-run business site, generally between 900 and 3,000 euros a year across all items; an e-commerce can exceed 4,000 euros. These are indicative values.

Can I do the maintenance myself?

For a simple site yes, if you have time and skills. For integrations or e-commerce a technician is better.

What happens if I do not pay for maintenance?

The site stays online but stops being updated and checked: the risks grow over months.

Does the fee include hosting and domain?

It depends on the contract: it must be written down. What matters is that domain and logins are yours.
