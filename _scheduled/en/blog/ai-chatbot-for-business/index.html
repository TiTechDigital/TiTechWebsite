AI chatbot for businesses: how to automate customer service

A chatbot can answer in seconds the questions you get every day and leave people the cases that matter. It can also get things wrong. Here is what it does well, what it does not, and how to introduce it without risk.

Talk about your project AI automation

Enea MujaCEO, TiTech Digital · 7 min readUpdated 3 December 2026

In brief

  • It answers repeated questions well. Hours, services, availability, how to order.
  • It can be wrong. For anything delicate it hands over to a person.
  • Privacy must be thought out first. Which data goes in, where it goes.
  • Start with one small case. And measure.

What an AI chatbot can do

A few well-defined tasks.

  • Frequent questionsHours, services, rough prices, address, how to book.
  • Collecting enquiriesAsks for name, need and contact and alerts the team.
  • Out of hoursReplies and takes note while the business is closed.
  • Support for peoplePrepares draft replies that an operator reviews.

The limits, stated clearly

Better to know first.

  • It can give wrong answers: for exact prices, contracts, health or legal matters it must hand over to a person.
  • It does not know your business unless you give it the right information.
  • The language must be tested with real customer questions.

Privacy and customer data

Before going live.

A chatbot receives what people type. This must be stated in the privacy notice, you must decide which data it may collect and how long it is kept, and health or financial data should be kept out of messages. If it uses outside services you need to know and say so.

How to start

Four steps.

  • Pick the 10-20 most frequent questions.
  • Write the right answers and the cases where it hands over to a person.
  • Test it with your team on real questions.
  • Measure how many requests it solves and where it fails.

Frequently asked questions

Does a chatbot replace customer service?

No: it removes repeated questions and leaves people the ones that need judgement.

Does it work on WhatsApp too?

It can, but WhatsApp Business has rules, verification and conditions for messages: we check them before building.

How much does it cost?

It depends on channels, amount of content and integrations. We send a written proposal after understanding the case.

Does it respect privacy?

It depends on how it is designed: data collected, suppliers and notice are decided first.
