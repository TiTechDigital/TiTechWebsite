How to digitalise a small business: a practical guide

Digitalising does not mean buying software: it means letting computers do the repetitive work and making the business easy to find and contact. A small business starts small, one step at a time.

Start from a concrete problem

Not from a technology.

Ask yourself: where do we lose most time? Where do we lose enquiries or customers? What do we write by hand several times? The digitalisation of a small business starts from these questions, not from a list of tools.

Five steps, in this order

A reasonable path.

  • 1. Be foundA clear website and a looked-after Google profile: see website for small businesses.
  • 2. Collect enquiriesA form and WhatsApp that bring contacts into one place.
  • 3. Keep customers and quotes in orderA shared sheet or a simple CRM: what a CRM is.
  • 4. Automate the repetitiveConfirmations, reminders, copying data: see AI automation for SMEs.
  • 5. MeasureWhere customers come from and what works.

How to choose tools

Few, and really used.

A well-designed tool that nobody uses is a cost. Better a few simple tools, chosen with the people who will use them. When no ready-made tool fits, consider custom software.

Mistakes to avoid

Three things.

  • Buying everything at once and using none of it.
  • Digitalising a confused process without simplifying it first.
  • Not training the people who will use it.

Note: if you hear about incentives or grants, always check the official calls and your accountant; we do not promise grants here.

Frequently asked questions

Where do I start digitalising?

By being found (website and Google profile) and by collecting enquiries in one place.

How much does it cost to digitalise a small business?

It depends on what you need. You can start small and grow. For a website see Indicative ranges for the Italian market: a showcase site between 800 and 2,500 euros, a business site between 2,000 and 6,500 euros, an e-commerce between 4,000 and 15,000 euros. The cost depends on the scope. For a quick estimate use the website cost calculator.

Do I need technical skills?

Not to start: tools are chosen so that you and your team can use them.

Do I need artificial intelligence?

Only where it solves a real problem. In many cases a simple rule is enough.
