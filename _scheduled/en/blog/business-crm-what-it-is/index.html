Business CRM: what it is and how it helps manage customers

A CRM is the place where a company keeps its customers, enquiries and what was said to each, instead of scattering them across email, phone and spreadsheets. It is not for everyone and not always a big piece of software: here is when it helps.

What a CRM is and what it does

Customer Relationship Management, in plain words.

A business CRM records for each customer or contact who they are, where they came from, what they asked, when to call back and where a deal stands. Everyone in the company sees the same information and nobody asks twice.

When a small business needs one

Four signs.

  • Enquiries get lostThey arrive by form, phone and WhatsApp and nobody knows who replies.
  • Several people talk to the same customersYou need a shared history.
  • Follow-ups are forgottenQuotes sent and never chased.
  • You want to know what worksWhere your best customers come from.

CRM, spreadsheet or standard software?

Three routes.

  • Spreadsheet: fine at the start, with few customers and one person.
  • Standard software: covers many cases, but you adapt to the program.
  • Custom: makes sense if your process is unusual or must connect to the site and other systems. For the comparison read custom web applications.

Connecting the CRM to the website

Enquiries should arrive on their own.

The site's contact form should send the enquiry into the CRM (or at least a shared inbox) with the source recorded, without retyping. Where a custom connection is needed, see custom software.

Frequently asked questions

Does a small business need a CRM?

Not always. If customers are few and one person remembers them all, a sheet is enough. When enquiries grow or several people handle them, a CRM helps.

How much does a CRM cost?

It depends on users and features: from a monthly subscription to custom software. A written quote clarifies what is included.

Is customer data safe?

It depends on the supplier and how you set it up. You need per-person access, backups and a clear privacy notice.

Can I connect the CRM to the website?

Yes, usually with an integration from the contact form.
