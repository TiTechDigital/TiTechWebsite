Business website: what the price includes and how to read the quote

Two quotes for a business website can differ a lot without one being "more expensive" than the other: what they include changes. Here is how to read the price line by line and what to check, without repeating the cost ranges you will find in the article on website costs.

Enea MujaCEO, TiTech Digital · 7 min readUpdated 10 December 2026

In brief

  • The total says little. What counts are the items included.
  • What is often missing. Text, photos, integrations, support after launch.
  • Ask what is excluded. And what adding it would cost.
  • Remember the yearly costs. Hosting, domain, maintenance.

What a price should include

Ten items to look for.

  • Pages and structureHow many pages and of what kind.
  • Phone-friendly designNot just for the computer.
  • Text and imagesWho writes and who supplies them.
  • Basic SEOTitles, descriptions, structure, redirects.
  • Contact form and measurementWith a privacy notice and respectful statistics.
  • Rounds of changesHow many are included.
  • Testing and launchOn phone and computer.
  • TrainingTo edit the content yourself.
  • Support after launchFor how long.
  • OwnershipDomain, code and content in your name.

What is often excluded

The items that push the cost up.

  • Professional text and photos, which are sometimes the biggest part of the work.
  • Translations into other languages.
  • Integrations with other systems.
  • Maintenance after the support period.

How to read a quote

Line by line.

Put the two quotes side by side and compare the same items. Where one is missing, ask whether it is excluded and what it would cost. A very low price often leaves out text, support or testing. For indicative values read how much does a website cost in Italy in 2026, and for costs after launch how much does website maintenance cost.

The questions before signing

Four.

  • What is included and what is not, in writing?
  • Who writes the text and who supplies it?
  • What yearly costs remain?
  • Who owns the domain and the logins?

Frequently asked questions

What does the price of a business website include?

Generally pages, phone-friendly design, basic SEO, a contact form, testing and launch. Text, photos, translations and integrations need checking.

How much does a business website cost?

Indicative ranges for the Italian market: a showcase site between 800 and 2,500 euros, a business site between 2,000 and 6,500 euros, an e-commerce between 4,000 and 15,000 euros. The cost depends on the scope. For a quick estimate use the website cost calculator.

Why do two quotes differ so much?

Because they include different items. Compare them line by line.

Is the text included?

It depends: always ask who writes it and how many rounds of changes are included.
