How much does an e-commerce cost in 2026?

The cost of an online store is not just the price of the site: there are the platform, payments, shipping and costs that return every year. Here are the items to count, an indicative range and how not to be surprised.

Talk about your project E-commerce

Enea MujaCEO, TiTech Digital · 8 min readUpdated 4 December 2026

In brief

  • The site is only a part. Payments, shipping and operations count too.
  • Indicative ranges: 4,000 to 15,000 euros. For an e-commerce, in our estimates.
  • There are recurring costs. Platform, fees, maintenance.
  • The margin decides whether it pays. Calculate it before opening.

The items to count

Six.

  • Platform and siteA ready-made solution or a custom store: see Shopify vs a custom website.
  • CatalogueHow many products, with which variants and photos.
  • PaymentsPer-transaction fees and supplier subscriptions.
  • Shipping and returnsRates, couriers and handling returns.
  • IntegrationsStock, invoicing, management software.
  • MaintenanceUpdates, security and support: see website maintenance.

Indicative ranges

For the Italian market, without promising.

Indicative ranges for the Italian market: a showcase site between 800 and 2,500 euros, a business site between 2,000 and 6,500 euros, an e-commerce between 4,000 and 15,000 euros. The cost depends on the scope. For a quick estimate use the website cost calculator. For yearly recurring costs, in our estimates an e-commerce can exceed 4,000 euros a year across platform, fees, support and updates.

The hidden costs

The ones found later.

  • Payment fees that eat into the margin.
  • Subscription apps and components that add up.
  • Product photos and text, often the biggest part.
  • Time to handle orders and returns, the cost that does not appear in a quote.

Margin first

Does it pay to open?

An online store makes sense if, after product cost, shipping, fees and time, a margin remains. Work it out with the e-commerce margin calculator before deciding, and see e-commerce for how we work. For a portal for companies read B2B e-commerce.

Frequently asked questions

How much does an online store cost?

In our estimates an e-commerce generally runs from about 4,000 to 15,000 euros, depending on catalogue, payments and integrations. The written quote gives the figure for your case.

Shopify or a custom site?

It depends on products, integrations and the ability to manage it. The comparison is in Shopify vs a custom website.

Are there costs every year?

Yes: platform or hosting, fees, maintenance and support.

Can I start with a few products?

Yes, and it is often the most prudent choice: you learn how it works before widening.
