How to choose an SEO agency for your company

SEO is work that only shows over time, and for that very reason it is easy to sell badly. Here is how to choose an SEO agency without being promised the impossible: what to ask, what to demand in writing and which warning signs not to ignore.

Talk about your project SEO consulting

Enea MujaCEO, TiTech Digital · 7 min readUpdated 10 December 2026

In brief

  • Nobody guarantees a position. Whoever does is a warning sign.
  • Ask for the method. What is done, in what order, how it is measured.
  • The access is yours. Search Console and Google profile in your name.
  • Demand clear reports. What changed and what comes next.

What an SEO agency really does

Four activities.

  • Analysis and technical baseIndexing, speed, structure, errors.
  • ContentPages and guides made for what people search.
  • Local SEOGoogle profile, reviews and consistent details: local SEO.
  • Measurement and reportsWhat changed and what is done the next month.

The questions to ask

Seven.

  • What exactly will you do in the first three months?
  • How do you measure the result, and with which tools?
  • What report will I get, and how often?
  • Who is my contact and who works on my site?
  • Who owns Search Console, the Google profile and the content?
  • How is the contract ended and what do I keep?
  • What techniques do you use for links from other sites?

The warning signs

Five.

  • Guaranteed positions or exact timing: nobody controls Google. See how long does it take to rank.
  • Bought links or "networks of sites".
  • No access to your own tools.
  • Unreadable reports or empty numbers only.
  • A very low price without explaining what is done.

What we do

Our way of working.

We set up a sound technical base, content made for real searches and, for those who work in an area, local presence. We measure with Search Console and promise no positions. The detail is in SEO consulting; for how to improve ranking read how to improve your ranking on Google.

Frequently asked questions

How do I choose an SEO agency?

By asking for method, reports and access in writing, and by distrusting anyone who promises positions or exact timing.

How much does SEO consulting cost?

It depends on what you need: an audit, a project or monthly work. Always ask for a list of activities, not just hours or a total.

Can I do SEO myself?

The basics yes (Google profile, content, speed). For the technical side and competitive searches an expert helps.

What should I demand from the agency?

Access in your name, clear reports, a method explained and no shortcut that puts the site at risk.
