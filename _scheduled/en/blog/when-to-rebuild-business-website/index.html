When is it time to rebuild your business website?

A website ages. But it does not always need rebuilding from scratch: sometimes fixing it is enough. Here is how to tell which case is yours and, if you rebuild, how not to lose the positions you already have on Google.

In brief

  • Five signs it needs rebuilding. Slow, poor on phones, impossible to edit, brings no enquiries, technologically old.
  • Sometimes optimising is enough. Content, speed, contact pages.
  • The migration must be protected. Redirects and URLs so rankings are not lost.
  • Rebuild with a goal. Not just to refresh the look.

The signs it needs rebuilding

Five.

  • It does not work well on a phoneTiny text, buttons hard to tap.
  • It is slowIt takes long to open even on a good connection, especially on a phone.
  • It cannot be editedChanging an opening time means calling someone.
  • It brings no enquiriesVisits without contacts: confusing pages, no clear action.
  • It is technologically oldComponents no longer updated: a security risk.

When optimising is enough

A rebuild is not always needed.

If the structure is sound but the text is old, the images heavy or the contact page confusing, you work on that. Rebuilding everything to change the style is a cost that often brings no extra enquiries. When in doubt, start from a check of the existing site: website design.

How not to lose Google positions

The most underestimated risk.

  • A map of addresses: list the pages that bring visits today.
  • 301 redirects from the old addresses to the new ones.
  • Keep the content that works, improving it instead of deleting it.
  • Check in Search Console after launch.

Nobody can guarantee a position on Google: you build the right foundations and measure the result over time.

Timing and costs

They depend on what changes.

Frequently asked questions

How often should a website be rebuilt?

There is no fixed deadline. It depends on how fast the technology ages and how much the business and its needs change.

Does rebuilding a site lose Google rankings?

It can if addresses and redirects are not handled. With a well-done migration the risk drops a lot, but it cannot be guaranteed.

Can I keep the old content?

Yes, and it is often advised for the pages that bring visits.
