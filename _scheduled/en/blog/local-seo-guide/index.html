Local SEO: how to get customers in your own city on Google

When someone searches "dentist Novara" or "pizzeria near me", Google shows a map with three profiles first. Local SEO is the work that gets you there and in the results below. Here is what to do, in order.

What local SEO is

Being found in an area.

Local SEO is about searches with a place, explicit ("Novara") or implicit ("near me"). What counts is the Google profile (the map), the website and what others say about you (reviews and citations). It applies to shops, practices, restaurants and anyone who receives customers or works in an area.

The three pillars

To look after together.

  • Google profileCorrect category, real hours, real photos, services and phone.
  • WebsiteA page per service, the area you work in and contacts: see SEO consulting.
  • Reviews and consistent detailsReviews from real customers, asked of everyone and without incentives; name, address and phone identical everywhere.

What to avoid

Four frequent mistakes.

  • Keywords in the profile name: it breaks Google's rules and can get the profile suspended.
  • Invented addresses or virtual offices.
  • Bought reviews or reviews you write yourself.
  • Photocopy pages with the city name changed.

How to measure

Calls and enquiries.

Look in the profile's statistics at how many calls and direction requests arrive, and in Search Console at which searches the site appears for. Ask new customers how they found you. Nobody can guarantee a position on Google: you build the right foundations and measure the result over time. For a practical plan see how to find customers online; for a concrete sector website for restaurants.

Frequently asked questions

What is local SEO?

The work that gets you into searches with a place, above all in Google's map and the results below.

Do I need a website if I have a Google profile?

Yes: the profile brings the first call, the website gives detail, proof and a fuller contact.

How long to appear?

For niche local searches sometimes a few weeks, for more competitive ones months. It cannot be guaranteed.

Can I do it myself?

The basics yes (profile, hours, photos, reviews). For the site and strategy an expert helps.
