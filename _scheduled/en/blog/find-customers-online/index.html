How to find customers online: strategies for small businesses

Finding customers online does not need a big budget, but it needs an order: first be found by people already searching, then be chosen, then measure. Here are the strategies that work best for a small business, and a simple plan to follow.

In brief

  • First, people already searching. Google profile and website answer someone with a need now.
  • Then reviews. They convince people choosing between you and someone else.
  • Social media help, but later. They build closeness, fewer immediate enquiries.
  • Measure. Where calls and enquiries come from.

Where customers look

Four places.

Someone with a need searches on Google and maps ("plumber Novara", "dentist near me"), asks acquaintances, looks at social media to get an idea and checks reviews before deciding. A strategy to find customers online covers these four places, in this order.

The tools, in order of return

For a small local business.

  • 1. A looked-after Google profileCategory, hours, real photos and phone: it is where many people decide the first call. See SEO consulting.
  • 2. A clear websiteServices, area, contacts and proof: website for small businesses.
  • 3. Real reviewsAsked of every customer, without incentives.
  • 4. Social mediaTo show your work: website or social media.
  • 5. Paid advertisingUseful once the rest works, never instead of the rest.

A plan for the first four weeks

Simple and realistic.

  • Week 1: complete Google profile, with hours and real photos.
  • Week 2: a site page for each main service, with the area you work in.
  • Week 3: ask recent customers for a review.
  • Week 4: see where calls and enquiries come from and adjust.

How to measure

Without numbers you are guessing.

Ask every new customer how they found you and write it down. Check in Google Search Console which searches the site appears for and on which page. Nobody can guarantee a position on Google: you build the right foundations and measure the result over time.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find my first customers online?

With a complete Google profile and a clear website that answer people already searching for your service in your area.

Do I need paid advertising?

Not at the start. It is better to be found for free first, then consider ads.

How long before I see results?

For niche local searches a few weeks; for more competitive ones, months. A time cannot be guaranteed.

What matters more, social media or the website?

For someone with a need now, the Google profile and website. Social media help you get known and chosen.
