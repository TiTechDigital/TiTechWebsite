How long does it take to rank on Google?

It is the question everyone asks, and the honest answer is: it depends. There is no time that fits everyone, and whoever promises one is selling something. Here is what it depends on and how to tell whether you are heading the right way.

Talk about your project SEO consulting

Enea MujaCEO, TiTech Digital · 6 min readUpdated 9 December 2026

In brief

  • It depends on three things. Competition, site age, work done.
  • Niche searches: faster. A few weeks, sometimes.
  • Competitive searches: months. And links from other sites.
  • No guarantee. Whoever promises page one is a warning sign.

What it depends on

Three factors.

  • CompetitionAppearing for "plumber + small town" is easier than for "website" in general.
  • Age and history of the siteA new site starts without reputation; one with years of work has an advantage.
  • Work doneTechnical base, content made for searches and, over time, links and reviews.

What to expect

Orders of magnitude, not promises.

For local or very specific searches, a well-kept site can start appearing after a few weeks or a few months. For generic, competitive searches it usually takes several months and continuous work. These are orders of magnitude, not guarantees: the position depends on Google and on competitors.

The signs of progress

Before positions.

  • Pages get indexed.
  • The first impressions appear in Search Console.
  • Clicks rise and so do direction requests on the profile.
  • Contacts arrive from people you did not know.

Who not to trust

A warning sign.

Nobody controls Google, so nobody can promise you first place. Be wary of anyone who gives you a date or a guaranteed position. For what a serious approach looks like see SEO consulting and how to improve your ranking on Google.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to appear on Google?

From a few weeks for niche local searches to several months for competitive ones. It depends on competition, site age and work done.

How long does a new site take?

It starts without reputation: it takes time to be indexed and known. A date cannot be promised.

Can anyone guarantee first position?

No. Nobody controls Google. Whoever promises it is a warning sign.

How can I speed it up?

By looking after the basics, with targeted pages, a Google profile and real reviews, and by avoiding shortcuts.
