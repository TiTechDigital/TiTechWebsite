<section class="sect sect--paper" aria-labelledby="faq-title">
  <div class="sect__top">
    <h2 class="m-text-4" id="faq-title">Before you write</h2>
  </div>
  <div class="faq">
    <details>
      <summary>What does a first conversation cost?</summary>
      <p>Nothing. Thirty minutes on video or on the phone to work out whether the project is within reach for both sides. If a deeper analysis is needed, with access to the systems, a check of the content and an estimate of timings, that is separate work with a cost we agree beforehand.</p>
    </details>
    <details>
      <summary>Do you take on small budgets?</summary>
      <p>Yes, if the work makes sense. A small showcase site of a few pages can sit in a contained band. What we say clearly is when a budget is not enough for the objective: in that case it is better to reduce the scope than to reduce the quality.</p>
    </details>
    <details>
      <summary>Do you reply even when we cannot work together?</summary>
      <p>Always. If the project is not our work, we write it with the reason: a technology outside our perimeter, an incompatible budget, an impossible deadline. A reasoned no is more useful than silence.</p>
    </details>
    <details>
      <summary>Can I write in English or Albanian?</summary>
      <p>Yes. We work and write in Italian, English and Albanian. The site is in Italian and English; project communication we handle in whichever language you prefer.</p>
    </details>
